Wavefronts of k vs G on a square lattice

Units: k in 2π/a, lattice spacing a = 1. Orange lines are the wavefronts k·r = 2πm (equal phase, through all space). Each atom is coloured by the phase k·R mod 2π that the wave has there: black ring = phase 0 (the atom sits on a wavefront). Every atom ringed ⇔ eik·R = 1 for all R ⇔ k is a reciprocal lattice vector G.
