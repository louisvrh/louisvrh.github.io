Systematic trading research (LTC/USDT)

Jul 2026 –

A Python trading pipeline with a backtester and walk-forward optimisation, paper-trading on live Binance data. Auditing the backtester, I fixed look-ahead bias and misaligned data windows, and found that the initial result (Sharpe 0.79 net of costs) did not hold up out of sample. I then added overfitting safeguards (a 25-trade minimum, the deflated Sharpe ratio) and used random-forest feature selection to cut the indicator set from 8 to 5.

Macro signal research: global trade and energy

Sep 2026 –

A platform that ingests 12 data sources daily across 25 economies (ship tracking, grid load, trade data), with a point-in-time store, a Streamlit dashboard and a walk-forward backtester. One finding: European power demand has decoupled from industrial output since 2022. Across 7 countries, the median correlation of year-on-year growth fell from 0.41 (2016–19) to −0.07 (2024–25).

Sequential Bayesian inference for radiation attenuation

Mar 2026

A computational physics project. I fit a nonlinear attenuation model to noisy count-rate data with MCMC (emcee; 64 walkers, 13k steps). Staged priors broke the degeneracy between 5 correlated parameters, bringing the Pearson ρ from 0.91 to below 0.5. Hypothesis testing rejected a single-exponential model (p ∼ 10⁻⁴) in favour of a bi-exponential one (p ≈ 0.12), and the source activity came out within 10% of the reference value.

Bayesian modelling of thermal waves

Dec 2025

A group project estimating the diffusivity and period-dependent reflectivity of thermal waves in a brass cylinder with MCMC. We found a degeneracy between reflectivity and diffusivity at short periods, which made single-point least-squares estimates unreliable. A reflected-wave model brought the reduced χ² from above 12.5 to 2.4 and gave a diffusivity within 3% of the literature value.
