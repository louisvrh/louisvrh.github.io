Sterile-neutrino search in MicroBooNE data

Oct 2026

A machine-learning research project testing the 3+1 sterile-neutrino explanation of the LSND and MiniBooNE anomalies through muon-neutrino disappearance. I selected charged-current νμ events with gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost, tuned with Optuna and 5-fold cross-validation), choosing the cut by expected sensitivity (82% purity, 94% efficiency), then fit the oscillation parameters (Δm², sin²2θ) with a χ² grid search and an overall normalisation nuisance parameter. I found no evidence for oscillation and set 90% CL limits (sin²2θ < 0.084 at Δm² = 3 eV²), excluding 5.2% of the LSND-allowed region. Above 3 eV² the sterile signal is degenerate with the overall normalisation, which weakens the limits by about 2.5× when the normalisation is left free.

Systematic trading research (LTC/USDT)

Jul 2026 –

A Python trading pipeline with a backtester and walk-forward optimisation, paper-trading on live Binance data. Auditing the backtester, I fixed look-ahead bias and misaligned data windows, and found that the initial result (Sharpe 0.79 net of costs) did not hold up out of sample. I then added overfitting safeguards (a 25-trade minimum, the deflated Sharpe ratio) and used random-forest feature selection to cut the indicator set from 8 to 5.

Sequential Bayesian inference for beta-radiation attenuation

Mar 2026

A computational physics project. I fit a two-component attenuation model to Sr-90 beta-radiation count-rate data with sequential Bayesian inference (emcee; 64 walkers, 13k steps), validated against Geant4 Monte Carlo simulations. Staged, simulation-calibrated priors brought the correlations between the attenuation coefficients below |ρ| = 0.5 and reduced the free parameters to three, while a degeneracy between the activity and the spectral fraction persisted (|ρ| ≈ 0.91). A single-exponential model was rejected (p ∼ 10⁻⁴) in favour of a bi-exponential one (p ∼ 0.12), confirming spectral hardening, and the measured activity of (4.18 ± 0.43) MBq is consistent with the quoted 4.4 MBq.

Bayesian modelling of thermal waves

Dec 2025

A group project estimating the diffusivity and period-dependent reflectivity of thermal waves in a brass cylinder with MCMC. We found a degeneracy between reflectivity and diffusivity at short periods, which made single-point least-squares estimates unreliable. A reflected-wave model brought the reduced χ² from above 12.5 to 2.4 and gave a diffusivity within 3% of the literature value.
